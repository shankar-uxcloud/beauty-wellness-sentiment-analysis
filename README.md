# Beauty & Wellness Customer Review Sentiment Analysis

A data science case study exploring how customer reviews of beauty products
can be analyzed to identify sentiment, recurring feedback patterns, and
potential business insights.

## Case Study Scope

This project follows a structured data science workflow:

1. Business Problem Definition
2. Public Data Source Identification
3. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing Strategy
4. Exploratory Data Analysis & Feature Engineering
5. Predictive Modeling & Algorithm Selection
6. Model Evaluation & Business Impact

## Domain

Beauty & Wellness

## Focus

Customer Review Sentiment Analysis
